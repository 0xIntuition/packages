Also, another important distinction is what the hub and spoke mean here. Hub is basically a locker that locks the token that we are bridging, let's say USDC from base. Spoke is the representation of this token on the other chain.

The practical consequence of this is that, when bridging spokes (which are the presentations of these tokens on our network), you don't need an approval, actually. That is something to take into account. You don't need to approve the USDC MetaERC20 spoke to itself, and same goes for wrapped it. Also, obviously, for the MetaNative spoke (which is used for Trust and Intuition), you obviously don't need to approve the native token. That goes without saying.

Slight UX bonus: don't let user wrap entire ETH balance on Base and entire TRUST balance on Intuition; let them keep at least 0.0005 ETH for gas and 1 TRUST for gas, respectively. Same goes for the ETH swaps on Base: cap it to max 0.0005 ETH left for gas etc.
